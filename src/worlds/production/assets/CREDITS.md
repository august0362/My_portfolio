# Production artwork

The owner supplied `data/Production/chess_ai_step3_layers_4k.zip` for this portfolio implementation. Its README and manifest identify the chess scene, reconstructed clean plate, three extracted floating pieces, and dust overlay. These are concept illustrations, not gameplay screenshots. No Genshin artwork, branding, icons, or map assets are used.

Public derivatives in `chess-ai/` were encoded with Sharp without EXIF, XMP, ICC, or IPTC metadata. Full and lighter backgrounds and transparent sprites preserve the manifest's common 3840 × 2160 geometry. `chess-concept.webp` comes from the supplied recomposition preview; `environment-study.webp` comes from the supplied alternative clean plate. The source ZIP remains unchanged and outside Git.

Permission is the owner's instruction to use the supplied artwork in this project; no separate third-party license was supplied. Icons are original inline SVG paths. Typography uses the application's existing Cormorant Garamond and Be Vietnam Pro fonts.
